Science Behind It

METHODOLOGY

Data-Driven Precision

Watt.Wise is built on a rigorous scientific foundation. Every reading, every forecast, and every recommendation is grounded in real electrical engineering principles, machine learning models trained on millions of Indian household patterns, and verified against DISCOM billing data.

Our disaggregation engine achieves greater than 94% appliance identification accuracy in controlled tests, and our bill forecasts consistently land within 3% of actual DISCOM bills across all major tariff slabs.

Measurement Standard IEC 61000-4-30 Class A power quality compliance for voltage and current sensing
Disaggregation Engine NILM (Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring) with a Factorial Hidden Markov Model trained on 2.4M Indian household records
Bill Forecasting Slab-wise tariff engine covering 180+ DISCOM rate structures across India, updated quarterly
Carbon Intensity State-specific CO2 emission factors sourced from Central Electricity Authority (CEA) annual reports
Data Sampling High-frequency sampling at 1-second intervals; aggregated into minute, hourly, and daily views
Security AES-256 encryption in transit and at rest. ISO 27001 certified infrastructure. GDPR-aligned data governance.
